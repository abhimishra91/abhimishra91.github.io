## Context

Global operations teams spent hours every day reading and sorting incoming email before any real work could start.

## The challenge

Build something from nothing: prove that NLP could reliably understand operational email, then turn a prototype into a service teams would trust with their daily workflow.

## What I did

- **Led the 0 → 1 initiative** as an innovation SME, from identifying the opportunity to rollout.
- **Built a deep-learning email classifier** and **served it as a web service** so it could plug into existing operational workflows.
- **Helped teams adopt it**, alongside broader automation and analytics initiatives.

## Outcome

The system removed **18 hours of manual effort every day** across global operations — and became the first of many automation and AI projects.
